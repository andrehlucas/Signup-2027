# Opus Virtual Offices — Structured Overview

## 1. What Is Opus Virtual Offices?

Opus Virtual Offices is a virtual office service provider founded in 2010 and headquartered in Boca Raton, Florida. It provides businesses with a professional corporate presence — including a real business address, live receptionist services, and a dedicated phone number — without the cost of renting physical office space. The company operates across **650+ locations** in the United States, Canada, and Puerto Rico.

Opus is designed for remote-first businesses, freelancers, startups, and service professionals who need credibility and infrastructure without overhead.

---

## 2. Core Services

### 2.1 Professional Business Address
- Access to 650+ prestigious mailing addresses in prime commercial locations across North America.
- Addresses can be used for LLC/business registration, branding, marketing materials, and official correspondence.
- Provides a verifiable commercial presence without leasing office space.

### 2.2 Live Receptionist (Unlimited)
- Every Opus plan includes **unlimited live call answering** by trained, professional receptionists.
- Calls are answered in your company's name during standard business hours.
- Receptionists can transfer calls, take messages, and follow custom call-handling scripts.
- This is Opus's flagship feature and primary differentiator.

### 2.3 Dedicated Local Phone & Fax Number
- Each client receives a **local phone number** and a **fax number** tied to their chosen location.
- Numbers are listed in 411 directory assistance for added legitimacy.
- Outgoing calls can be placed from the business number via the Opus mobile app.

### 2.4 Voicemail-to-Email
- Voicemail messages are automatically converted and delivered as audio files to your email inbox.
- Enables quick review and response without dialing into a voicemail system.

### 2.5 Digital Fax-to-Email
- Incoming faxes are received digitally and delivered as PDF attachments to your email.
- No physical fax machine required.

### 2.6 Mail Handling & Forwarding
- Mail is received at your business address and forwarded to your designated personal or home address on a pre-set schedule.
- Some locations offer mail scanning services.

### 2.7 Opus Mobile App
- Manage calls, voicemails, faxes, and messages from your smartphone.
- Place outgoing calls that display your Opus business number (not your personal number).

### 2.8 Meeting Room Access
- Select premium locations offer **conference and meeting room rentals** at hourly rates (typically $25/hour).
- Some locations also include private parking.
- Availability varies by location — most locations are mail-center-based and do not include meeting rooms.

### 2.9 Business Credit Building
- Opus reports your monthly payment history to **CreditSafe** and **Moody's**.
- This helps new businesses establish and build business credit — a feature not commonly offered by competitors.

## 4. Key Differentiators

### 4.1 All-Inclusive Live Receptionist
This is Opus's single biggest differentiator. **Every plan includes unlimited live call answering** at no extra cost. Most competitors either:
- Don't offer live receptionist services at all in their base plans.
- Charge it as a premium add-on that can cost $50–$150/month on its own.
- Cap the number of receptionist minutes per month.

For businesses that rely on phone calls (consultants, contractors, legal professionals, real estate agents), this alone can justify the $99/month price.

### 4.2 Simple, All-In-One Pricing
- No confusing tier structure. One plan, one price, everything included.
- Reduces decision fatigue and eliminates surprise costs from add-on stacking.
- Trade-off: you pay for live answering even if you don't need it.

### 4.3 Business Credit Reporting
- Monthly payments are reported to CreditSafe and Moody's, helping businesses build a credit profile.
- This is rare among virtual office providers and valuable for new LLCs and startups seeking future financing.

### 4.4 Outbound Calling via Mobile App
- The mobile app lets you make outgoing calls that display your Opus business number.
- Maintains professional separation between personal and business communications.

### 4.5 411 Directory Listing
- Your business phone number is listed in 411 directory assistance, adding a layer of legitimacy and discoverability.

## 7. What Opus Does NOT Offer

- **Physical office space or coworking** — no desks, no hot-desking, no dedicated offices.
- **Inventory or equipment storage** — addresses are for mail only.
- **Tiered or budget plans** — no lower-cost option if you don't need live receptionist services.
- **Extensive global coverage** — primarily North America (US, Canada, Puerto Rico).

## 8. Customer Feedback Summary

### Positives
- Professional, courteous live receptionists.
- Quick and easy setup (typically within 1 business day).
- Good value for businesses that actively use the phone answering service.
- Mobile app is convenient for managing communications on the go.

### Common Complaints
- ID verification and approval process can be slow or frustrating for some applicants.
- The 3-month auto-renewal with a 30-day cancellation window catches some customers off guard.
- No flexibility to pay less if you don't need the live receptionist feature.
- Meeting room availability is limited to a small number of locations.

### Review Presence
- **Trustpilot**: 244+ reviews, mixed ratings.
- **BBB**: Accredited since 2014.

---

## 9. Summary

Opus Virtual Offices occupies a clear niche in the virtual office market: **the all-inclusive option for businesses that need live call answering bundled with a professional address.** Its flat $99/month pricing makes it straightforward, and the included receptionist service is its standout feature — most competitors charge significantly more for equivalent call handling.

It is best suited for small businesses, startups, and solo professionals who want a polished corporate image and reliable phone coverage without the complexity of managing multiple vendor add-ons. For businesses that only need a mailing address or digital mailbox, lower-cost alternatives like iPostal1 or Northwest Registered Agent may be a better fit.
