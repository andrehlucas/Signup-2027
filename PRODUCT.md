## Platform

web

## Users

Businesses buying a virtual office solution for remote work.

## Product Purpose

Opus Virtual Offices helps businesses establish a professional business presence and handle business communications without leasing a dedicated full-time office. Success means customers can understand the available service, select a location and plan, customize optional services, and complete enrollment.

## Positioning

The flagship virtual office offer is presented as an all-inclusive solution with no hidden fees, combining a business address with live receptionist and communication services. Do not extend that positioning to the separate address-only offer or imply that every service, location, or pass-through charge is included.

## Operating Context

Customers enroll online by providing business contact information, choosing a location and plan, selecting optional services, and reviewing payment and agreement terms. Location availability and services can vary.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- A virtual office provides a professional business address and communication services; it is not a dedicated full-time office.
- The flagship offer is currently advertised at $99/month across 650+ U.S. locations. Its stated inclusions are a business address, mail receipt, live call answering, personalized call transferring, business phone/fax numbers, and voicemail/fax delivered by email. The phone setup includes two voicemail boxes and transfers that can ring up to four customer-supplied numbers.
- Address-only is a separate offer, currently advertised at $59/month across 100+ locations. It includes a business address and mail/package receipt; do not attribute the flagship offer's phone or receptionist features to it.
- Live answering hours are stated as Monday–Friday, 8:30 a.m.–5:30 p.m., with Pacific hours listed as 8 a.m.–5 p.m. Confirm the applicable time zone before publishing hours.
- The public FAQ describes an initial three-month agreement, monthly renewal afterward, and 30 days' written notice to cancel. “No long-term contract” must not be presented as “no agreement” or as cancellation without notice. The current checkout UI exposes other term choices; confirm approved commercial terms before changing or publishing them.
- Mail pickup is free. Forwarding is available at most locations and incurs postage/shipping charges. Mail acceptance requires approval of the required USPS documentation.
- Meeting rooms are available only at selected premium locations and are charged separately by the hour.
- Outbound calling, texting, toll-free numbers, custom email, call recording/logs, supervised transfers, message taking, and appointment scheduling are optional upgrades, not automatic inclusions in the $99 offer.
- Custom Business Email is an optional $10/month add-on. The user-supplied offer includes custom addresses on the business domain, domain registration, 10gb secure email hosting, and access from any device. Do not imply that selecting it in the prototype provisions a domain or mailbox.
- Do not promise universal bank approval or business-registration eligibility; qualify these claims.
- Recheck prices, promotions, setup fees, and location counts against current approved sources before publication.

## Brand Commitments

- Use the product name Opus Virtual Offices.
- Preserve the confirmed flagship positioning as an all-inclusive virtual office offer, while clearly distinguishing included services from optional, location-dependent, or separately charged services.

## Evidence on Hand

- The user reports verifying the current offer details against the Opus Virtual Offices public website, including its homepage and FAQ: https://opusvirtualoffices.com. Treat the listed prices, promotions, hours, and location counts as time-sensitive and recheck them before publication.
- The project includes the Opus Virtual Offices logo at `public/opus-vo-main-logo.svg`.
- No customer testimonials, universal eligibility guarantees, or other independent proof claims have been confirmed for reuse.

## Product Principles

1. Clearly distinguish the flagship virtual office offer from the address-only offer.
2. State what is included, optional, location-dependent, and separately charged.
3. Describe the service as a business address and communications solution, not a dedicated office.
4. Qualify claims about banking, business registration, mail handling, and service availability.
5. Verify time-sensitive commercial details before publishing them.
