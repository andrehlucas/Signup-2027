---
name: Official office photo sourcing
description: Preserve the identity of each specific office when choosing location-card photos.
---

Use the primary gallery photo from the exact official office page supplied by the user, not a generic city image, stock office photo, or a different branch in the same city.

**Why:** The user provided specific office URLs and requested each location's main photo. Matching only the city can associate a photo with the wrong business address.

**How to apply:** Verify the official location ID, street address, suite, and first gallery image together. Keep the source page available for provenance when refreshing photos. Do not silently substitute another office's photo if the source is unavailable.
